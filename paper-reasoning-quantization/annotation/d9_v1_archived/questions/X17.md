```
  1| ### Outcome-to-Transition Table
  2| 
  3| The following table maps each `ConsensusVerdict.outcome` value to its next state transition and escalation reason (if applicable). This is the canonical reference for outcome routing.
  4| 
  5| | `ConsensusVerdict.outcome` | Next phase / state | Escalation reason | Notes |
  6| |----------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------|
  7| | `consensus` | Phase 5 (Resolution Formation) | — | All participants agreed. Proceed to artifact production. |
  8| | `conditional` | Phase 5 (Resolution Formation) | — | Concerns present but nothing blocks. Record reservations and proceed. |
  9| | `no-consensus` | Phase 3 (re-enter discussion round) | — | Budget has not been exhausted; additional discussion rounds may resolve the issue. |
 10| | `blocked-escalation` | `awaiting-operator` | `information-gap` \| `irresolvable-conflict` \| `risk-threshold-breach` | Escalation protocol triggered. See §Escalation Protocol below. |
 11| 
 12| Where:
 13| - `no-consensus` applies when `no_consensus` budget remains (phase_state transitions 4→3 per runtime contract).
 14| - When budget is exhausted, the effective outcome is equivalent to `blocked-escalation` with `reason = information-gap`.
 15| - Missing Core role or invalid-format status also routes to `blocked-escalation` with the appropriate reason, checked independently of this table.
```
